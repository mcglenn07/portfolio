### Priority Issues

- **[P1] Invisible/near-invisible text on at least one color pair**: What: the detector found 16 low-contrast instances the manual review missed, including six at **1.0:1** (`#ffffff` on `#f0fcfe`) — text that is functionally invisible against its background. Why it matters: whatever element(s) use this pair are unreadable for every visitor, not just low-vision users; this is a correctness bug, not a taste call. Fix: search style.css for `#f0fcfe` background rules and confirm what text color renders on them (likely a light-on-light state that should invert, e.g. a hover/active state color leaking into a default state). Suggested command: `/impeccable audit`.

- **[P1] Mobile nav toggle communicates no state**: What: `#navToggle` toggles `.open` on `#navLinks` but never sets `aria-expanded`, has no `aria-controls`, and there's no visual hamburger→X transform. Why it matters: keyboard/screen-reader users get zero confirmation the menu opened — this is the first interactive control on mobile, where a large share of portfolio traffic lands. Fix: toggle `aria-expanded="true|false"` in the click handler, add `aria-controls="navLinks"`, add a visual open-state transform, and add outside-tap/Escape dismissal (currently only a nav-link click closes it). Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`.

- **[P1] Filter bar declares `role="tablist"` without the rest of the ARIA tab pattern**: What: `#projectFilterBar` has `role="tablist"`, but the pills aren't `role="tab"`/`aria-selected` and the panels aren't `role="tabpanel"`. Why it matters: a screen-reader user hears "tab list" then gets no tab semantics or arrow-key roving support — a declared-but-unfulfilled pattern is worse than none. Fix: implement the full ARIA APG tabs pattern, or drop `role="tablist"` and let the native buttons speak for themselves. Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`.

- **[P2] Repeated "kicker-above-heading" pattern across every section**: What: all 7 sections use an identical small-label-above-h2 structure (About→"Software Engineer...", Work→"Selected Projects", etc.), confirmed by both the static and live-DOM scans. Why it matters: this is a recognizable generic-AI-design tell that works against the site's otherwise strong specificity — it's the kind of pattern that makes a genuinely personal site read as templated at a glance. Fix: vary the pattern (drop kickers on 2-3 sections, or replace with something more section-specific than a generic eyebrow label). Suggested command: `/impeccable typeset`.

- **[P2] `#skills` breaks the page's own design discipline**: What: 6 groups, 42 tag chips total, 4 groups exceeding 8+ items (well past the ≤4-item chunking guideline), every chip identical visual weight. Why it matters: it's the page's one cognitive-load and aesthetic failure, sitting right after the strongest section (Projects) and pulling the emotional arc down before Education/Testimonials recover it. Fix: cap visible chips per group (~5-6) with a "+N more" or promote a "core stack" callout above the rest. Suggested command: `/impeccable distill`.
